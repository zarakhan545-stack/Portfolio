§ 03

Standalone research project

Causal-Aware Multi-Agent Fraud Detection

A reproducible research implementation for digital financial fraud detection using three calibrated predictive agents, model-disagreement and uncertainty signals, and a simulated human-review stage. The evaluation design includes no-review, entropy-only, disagreement-only, and combined decision-policy baselines.

Methods: class-weighted Logistic Regression, Random Forest and HistGradientBoosting; train/validation/test separation; calibration; PR-AUC, ROC-AUC, Brier score, precision, recall, F1, false-positive rate, review rate, bootstrap intervals and sensitivity analysis.

Dataset: ULB/Worldline credit-card fraud benchmark. Raw data is not stored in the repository.

Current status: reproducible implementation and experimental pipeline are in the repository. Full execution and validation on the real dataset remain pending; no empirical performance results are claimed.
